# BULOS 3D: Brand Identity & Guidelines

**Xylem Global Student Innovation Challenge · Camarines Sur National High School**  
Pilot Region: *Mt. Isarog Catchment → Naga River System → Naga City, Camarines Sur*

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Name Origin

### The Name: **BULOS 3D**
* **"Bulos" (Bikol / Tagalog)**: Refers to the vigorous surge, current, and continuous flow of freshwater rushing down mountain watersheds and volcanic springs. It embodies life-giving water, momentum, and continuous vitality.
* **"3D"**: Represents the spatial hydrodynamic digital twin — integrating Copernicus DEM 3D topography, real-time 3D particle transport, and Three.js isometric asset diagnostics.

### Brand Tagline
> **"Safeguarding every drop before it reaches the tap."**  
> *Anticipatory water security, IoT surrogate analytics, and autonomous protection for rural networks.*

### Mission Statement
To turn existing and low-cost IoT telemetry into an intelligent digital twin that detects contamination from surrogate sensors, forecasts arrival hours ahead, and shuts off communal valves before contaminated water can reach vulnerable families.

## 2. Logo Design & Anatomy

The **BULOS 3D** brand mark fuses three core concepts:

```
        ▲  Volcanic Watershed Contour (Mt. Isarog GLO-90 DEM)
       / \
      / ~ \  Dynamic Water Current (Bulos Flow & Advection Waves)
     |  ●  | Digital Twin Core (LoRaWAN IoT Sensor & Valve Interlock)
      \___/
```

1. **The Dynamic Water Droplet**: The universal symbol of clean drinking water, sculpted with a modern, aerodynamic tear silhouette.
2. **Topographic Watershed Contours**: Layered inner wave curves echoing the 1,852 m to 4 m volcanic elevation profile of Mt. Isarog down to the Naga River.
3. **The Digital Twin Telemetry Node**: The central illuminated core with concentric radio rings, representing autonomous edge sensors (pH, turbidity, TDS, ORP, TLF biological fluorescence) and LoRaWAN mesh communication.

## 4. Typography

* **Display / Brand Wordmark**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Outfit` (Bold / ExtraBold, letter-spacing -0.5px) — modern, friendly yet precise engineering aesthetic.
* **Body & UI**: `Inter` or System Sans-Serif (`-apple-system`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`) — high legibility across mobile and desktop dashboards.
* **Telemetry & Sensor Numbers**: `JetBrains Mono` or tabular figures — alignment of decimal readings (e.g. `2.4 NTU`, `405 mV`, `7.35 pH`).
