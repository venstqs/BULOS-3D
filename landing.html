Your water looks clean⁠

This is the real Naga River, from Mt. Isarog to Naga City. Click it to spill something, and watch what its digital twin does.

Simulated spill+0 min
    Step 1 of 5

    Sense⁠

    One biological sensor sits upstream. Low-cost nodes watch every water point below it.

    Step 2 of 5

    Sync⁠

    Every reading updates the virtual river, so the model stays in step with the water.

    Step 3 of 5

    Simulate⁠

    River physics runs four hours ahead to show where polluted water is going, and when.

    Step 4 of 5

    Decide⁠

    Risk rules judge each point. They can tell a dirty probe from real pollution.

    Step 5 of 5

    Act⁠

    Intakes close before the water arrives, and stored water takes over. That is what makes it a twin, not a map.

    Honest about what this is⁠

    Bulos 3D is a working software prototype by students. No sensor hardware has been built yet, and every water reading on this page is simulated.
